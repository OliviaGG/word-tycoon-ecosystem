How Scoring Works

Your score is built from three layers that stack together. Points and coins earned are equal.

Word Rarity

Base points from word type

  • Common: 500
  • Rare: 1,000
  • Ultra Rare: 1,500
  • 3-Letter: 100

Letter Rarity

Each letter adds a bonus

  • Common (E,T,A...): +0
  • Uncommon (D,L): +15
  • Rare (C,U,M,W,F): +30
  • Epic (G,Y,P): +60
  • Legendary (B,V,K): +105
  • Mythic (J,X): +180
  • Unique (Q,Z): +300

Word Length Bonus

Longer words earn exponential bonus (3-letter exempt)

4 letters: +75 · 5: +150 · 6: +300 · 7: +500 · 8: +800 · 9: +1,200 · 10+: up to +3,500

Daily recTangled as a Squaredle alternative

recTangled Solo is aimed at players who enjoy the daily Squaredle rhythm but want a different board, a different scoring feel, and a clear rarity ladder for the words they find. The fixed grid keeps the experience easy to understand while still rewarding deeper searches.

Best for daily puzzle players

Open the puzzle, sweep the board for quick common words, then keep digging for rare and ultra-rare finds that move the score much harder.

Best for archive players

If you are searching for more than one board to play, recTangled's daily-and-archive structure gives you more depth than a single one-and-done session.

  • Like Squaredle, but distinct: adjacent-letter word hunting stays familiar, while the rarity system changes how you prioritize paths.
  • Free browser play: no app install required to try a daily word search puzzle in the same overall genre.
  • Great for completionists: common, rare, and ultra-rare buckets make it easier to tell whether you found the obvious layer or the deep layer of the board.
