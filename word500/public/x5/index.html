Five words. One puzzle.

Find five crossing five-letter answers using 25 guesses. Type one word: it goes into all five spots at once and costs one guess.

  • Green: letters in the right spot.
  • Yellow: letters in the word, in a different spot.
  • Red: letters absent from the word.

One triangle combines all 25 letter comparisons, including words already found. It gives counts, not individual letters or separate word scores. Letters can repeat.

Each word's tiles share its own match colour, scored against that answer: red for no matching letters, through yellow, to green for an exact answer. The colour describes the whole word, not individual letters.

At crossings, → shows the across copy and ↓ shows the down copy. Each reads the same guessed word independently and keeps its own word colour.

Every new guess replaces the previous board and triangle. The words-found count increases when you guess an exact answer. Find all five to win. Invalid and repeated guesses cost nothing.

Tap a board letter to add your own green, yellow or red note. Notes are your deductions and clear with the next guess.

Your progress saves in this browser. The daily changes at midnight in Edmonton; practice has fresh puzzles whenever you want.
