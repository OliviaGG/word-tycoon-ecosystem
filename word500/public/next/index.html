How to play

Guess the secret word. Type it and press enter — every guess has to be a real word.

Reading a guess

You're told how many letters you got, never which ones. The triangle beside each guess splits it three ways:

  • right letter, right spot
  • right letter, wrong spot
  • not in the word at all

The guess itself is tinted by how close you are — the greener the row, the better it scored. Letters can repeat.

Working it out

Tap any letter to colour it for yourself; tapping again cycles green → yellow → red → off. Tap a wedge of the triangle to colour a whole row at once, and double-tap that wedge to clear the row again. The marks are just your notes — nobody else sees them.

Daily & multiplayer

The DAILY is one word a day, the same for everyone, and it counts toward your streak. In multiplayer every guess in the room is shown as it lands, and the first to crack the word takes it.
