How to play

Each shift gives you six reusable letters. Forge words with 3–6 letters. The ledger shows every accepted word as a hidden slot, grouped by length. Each word scores once per shift.

Base points: 3 letters = 10 · 4 = 25 · 5 = 50 · 6 = 100. To bank a shift, meet its score quota and find at least one six-letter blueprint. You can keep forging for a higher score before banking.

Choose one of three contracts between shifts. Contracts last for the rest of the run. In the final shift, every word must include the boss’s locked letter.

The daily workshop uses the same racks and contract offers for everyone and changes at midnight in Edmonton. Fresh runs use different racks. Practice removes the clock. Use Pause to take a break; leaving the tab pauses your shift automatically.

Scores and saves stay in this browser.
