MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

One word at a time.

Connect letters on the wheel to uncover words in the crossword. You can use each letter tile once per word, including separate tiles of the same letter.

  1. Swipe through letters and release to submit.
  2. Tap letters, then select Enter word.
  3. Type a word and press Enter. Backspace undoes; Escape clears; Space shuffles.

Adventure: 24 levels grow from tiny three-letter warmups to seven-letter expert crosswords. Complete a level to unlock the next.

Daily: a separate crossword for everyone, refreshed every day at midnight in Edmonton. Your daily save and streak stay separate from your adventure.

Select a crossword cell to focus a word; the sun button reveals one letter. Finish with no hints for three stars, up to three hints for two, or more hints for one.

Curated word lists, progress, extra words, and stars are saved in this browser.
