Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Word Tycoon and related Word Tycoon game experiences collect only the information needed to operate the games, save player progress, and support account-linked features.

Information We Collect

  • Account and profile information such as platform identifiers, usernames, display names, and profile images provided by connected platforms such as Reddit, Discord, TikTok, or other supported services.
  • Gameplay and account activity such as scores, guesses, unlocks, rewards, progression, and membership or purchase entitlements.
  • Technical and usage information needed to run and improve the service, such as basic logs, timestamps, connection status, diagnostics, browser or device identifiers, page and app interactions, and approximate location inferred from an IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for essential service operation and, where present, analytics or advertising measurement.

How We Use Information

  • To run Word Tycoon games and related puzzle experiences.
  • To save progress, leaderboards, rewards, and account-linked inventory.
  • To monitor reliability, investigate abuse, measure usage, and improve game features.

Sharing

Word Tycoon does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers, platform integrations, analytics or measurement providers, or infrastructure needed to operate and improve the games. These providers process information only for the services they supply.

Security

Word Tycoon uses reasonable safeguards to protect information. Data sent between supported apps or websites and the service is encrypted in transit.

Retention

Gameplay and account data may be retained as long as needed to operate the service, maintain progression, support moderation, and preserve backups.

Your Choices

If you want account data removed, follow the steps on our account deletion page. For other privacy questions, email Wordtycoon67@gmail.com.

Children

Word Tycoon is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes means the updated policy applies.
