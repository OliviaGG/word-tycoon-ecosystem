Delete your Word Tycoon account data

For Subtextle and other Word Tycoon games

You can request deletion of your Word Tycoon account and the data associated with it, including data used by Subtextle.

How to request deletion

  1. Email Wordtycoon67@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request.
  2. Tell us which game you used, such as Subtextle, and the sign-in provider and username or user ID connected to the account.
  3. Reply to any verification request so we can confirm that you own the account. Do not send your password.

What is deleted

After ownership is verified, we will delete or de-identify account and profile identifiers, connected-platform links, saved gameplay and progression, rewards, inventory, and other account-linked game activity from active systems.

What may be kept

Limited security, fraud-prevention, transaction or ledger, and legal-compliance records may be retained only when reasonably necessary for those purposes. Residual copies may remain in protected backups until those backups are overwritten under the normal backup schedule; backup data is not used to recreate a deleted account except when required for disaster recovery or legal compliance.

Timing

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Routine automated database backups generally rotate within 14 days, although limited incident or compliance records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.
