Concierge FAQ

Is it actually free?

Every game, every daily, every business — free. The Tycoon Pass ($2/month) adds unlimited solo play, online multiplayer in everything, batch guessing in stream chat, and voice perks. The elevator is free either way.

Do I need one account per game?

No — that's the whole point of the tower. One sign-in (Google, Discord, or GitHub) works across every floor. Win XP in Subtextle at breakfast, spend it on your Pizza Palace at lunch.

How do daily and weekly bonuses work?

Clear 9 daily games for the daily bonus and 5 weekly games for the weekly one. Streaks multiply your take. Miss a day and the butler will not say anything, but he will remember.

How big is the word bank?

7,101 hand-picked target words backed by a 177,107-word dictionary, so your weirdest legal guess still counts. The Q tile remains illiquid; we're working on it.

What's the GOAT board?

The all-time leaderboard across every game — XP, wins, and ELO where it applies. Reaching the top is statistically unlikely and emotionally mandatory.

Do my viewers need accounts?

Nope — they just type guesses in your TikTok chat. If they sign in, their guesses also pay XP into their own empire. Everybody profits. Especially you.
