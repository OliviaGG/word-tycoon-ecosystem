After creating each account, update the social links in `public/landing.html` footer with the real URLs.

### Bluesky Custom Domain (Optional)
You can verify `wordtycoon.live` as your Bluesky handle by adding a DNS TXT record:
- Record: `_atproto.wordtycoon.live`
- Value: `did=did:plc:<your-did>` (Bluesky provides this in Settings → Handle)
- Then your handle becomes `@wordtycoon.live` instead of `@wordtycoon.bsky.social`
