How to play

One hidden article. Find its title, one word at a time.

  1. 1

    Guess a word

    Type any word into the guess bar. Start broad, then follow what you discover.

  2. 2

    Follow the clues

    Words in the article reveal text. Lower ranks mean closer guesses to the highlighted title word—even if no text appears.

  3. 3

    Find the title

    Guess each hidden title word to solve the puzzle. When you find one, the ranks reset for the next word.
