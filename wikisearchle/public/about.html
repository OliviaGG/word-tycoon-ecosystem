A few helpful answers

Before you dive in

Why does a close guess reveal no text?

Rank measures meaning, while a reveal means the word appears in the article. A good rank is still a useful clue even when no text is revealed.

What earns points?

New words revealed in the article earn points. Title finds earn bonuses. A close semantic rank alone does not earn reveal points.

Is my Solo progress saved?

Solo progress is saved in this browser for each puzzle. Return on the same browser to continue. Previous daily games keep separate progress and do not award coins or career stats.

Do I need an account?

You can play the Daily without signing in. Unlimited random Solo play uses Donor Class access. Sign in through Word Tycoon for account rewards, profile stats, and cosmetics.

Where are my profile and cosmetics?

Open Profile or Cosmetics from the home menu. Your Word Tycoon account connects your progress and equipped style across games.
