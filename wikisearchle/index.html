WikiSearchle

Type words in the stream chat to uncover a hidden Wikipedia article. Rank-closest guesses climb the board — finders earn points.

How to Play

1
A Wikipedia article is chosen and every word is blacked out.
2
Viewers type any word in chat.
3
Each guess gets a semantic rank; closest climbs the Top Guesses cards.
4
Words that appear in the article are revealed; finders earn 1,000 pts each.
5
Round ends when someone guesses the target word.
