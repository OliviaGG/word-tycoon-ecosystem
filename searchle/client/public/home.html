How to Play

Subtextle is about meaning, not letters.

1 · Guess by meaning

A secret word is hidden. Type any word and the game tells you how semantically similar your guess is — not spelling, but meaning. You have unlimited guesses.

2 · Read the ranks

Every guess gets a rank. Lower is better — rank #1 is the word itself.

#1You found the word! 🎉
1–1,000Hot — very close in meaning
1K–10KWarm — getting closer
10K+Cool — not very related

3 · Think in concepts

If your guess is warm, try other words from the same topic or category. If the secret word is OCEAN, then WAVE lands near the top while TREE sits thousands of ranks away.
