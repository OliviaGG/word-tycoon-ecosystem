How Scoring Works

Your score is built from three layers that stack together. Points and coins earned are equal.

Word Rarity

Base points from word type

  • Common: 500
  • Rare: 1,000
  • Ultra Rare: 1,500
  • 3-Letter: 100

Letter Rarity

Each letter adds a bonus

  • Common (E,T,A...): +0
  • Uncommon (D,L): +15
  • Rare (C,U,M,W,F): +30
  • Epic (G,Y,P): +60
  • Legendary (B,V,K): +105
  • Mythic (J,X): +180
  • Unique (Q,Z): +300

Word Length Bonus

Longer words earn exponential bonus (3-letter exempt)

4 letters: +75 · 5: +150 · 6: +300 · 7: +500 · 8: +800 · 9: +1,200 · 10+: up to +3,500

erecTangled for Squaredle players

erecTangled sits in the same broad lane as Squaredle, but its hook is the expanding-ring board. Instead of treating the grid as fully revealed from the start, the puzzle opens outward and changes the way you scan, revisit, and prioritize paths.

What makes it different

The expanding layout creates phases inside one board, so the puzzle keeps changing as new rings appear and old assumptions stop being enough.

Why that matters

It gives word-search players a reason to keep adapting instead of only sweeping the same visible surface from the opening second.

  • Squaredle alternative with a twist: still a swipe-based letter-grid puzzle, but the expanding-ring mechanic changes the rhythm of the hunt.
  • Live rounds and rarity tiers: erecTangled adds more visible momentum for multiplayer and stream play.
  • Better for players who want novelty: if standard static grids feel solved too early, the growing board keeps pressure on.
