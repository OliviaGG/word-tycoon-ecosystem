How Scoring Works

Your score is built from three layers that stack together. Points and coins earned are equal.

Word Rarity

Base points by word type — the required board words pay most

  • Common (required): 1,500
  • Ultra Rare (hidden): 1,000
  • Rare (extra): 500
  • 3-Letter: 100

Letter Rarity

Every letter adds a bonus — none score zero

  • Common (E,T,A...): +25
  • Uncommon (D,L): +40
  • Rare (C,U,M,W,F): +60
  • Epic (G,Y,P): +90
  • Legendary (B,V,K): +140
  • Mythic (J,X): +220
  • Unique (Q,Z): +350

Word Length Bonus

Longer words earn exponential bonus (3-letter exempt)

4 letters: +75 · 5: +150 · 6: +300 · 7: +500 · 8: +800 · 9: +1,200 · 10+: up to +3,500

Daily erecTangled as a Squaredle alternative

For players searching for a Squaredle alternative, erecTangled Solo keeps the familiar adjacent-letter word-search foundation but adds a stronger sense of progression through expanding rings. That makes each daily board feel less static and more like a puzzle that unfolds.

Daily puzzle appeal

You still get the satisfying daily board habit, but the board shape changes the search rhythm as new space opens.

Why archive play works

The expanding mechanic gives older boards replay value because the route-finding process is less repetitive than a plain static scan.

  • Free browser puzzle: jump straight into a daily word-search game without installing anything.
  • Expanding-ring identity: the main differentiator versus Squaredle-style static boards is that the puzzle grows while you play.
  • Rarity-aware scoring: common, rare, and ultra-rare words make the deeper finds feel more deliberate.
