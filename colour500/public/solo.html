How to play

A secret code of four colours. Colours can repeat. You get ten guesses.

Reading the triangle

  • right color, right slot
  • right color, wrong slot
  • not in the code at all

It never tells you which slot — that's the whole game.

Daily vs practice

Everyone gets the same daily code, once a day. Solve it while signed in and it counts toward your streak and pays coins. Practice rounds are unlimited and pay nothing.
